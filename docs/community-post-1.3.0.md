## :test_tube: Widgetkeeper 1.3.0 is in testing

Everything from 1.2.1, and: https://homey.app/a/com.thomassidor.widgetkeeper/test/

* **Three new widgets:**
  * **Combined Value:** one value of several devices as one, such as the average temperature of every room or the lowest battery, with an OK range and each device against the others.
  * **Value Board:** every value of the devices you pick, grouped by room or device; what needs a look stands out by itself.
  * **Flow Tiles:** Device Values' tiles, written by your Flows: a text or number, an icon, a colour and a fill. A tap can start a Flow.
* **One time format for the whole app:** 24 or 12 hours, under **Configure → Time format**, now also on the charts, Electricity, the price strip, Weather and Timers.
* **155 more icons,** 201 in all, found by typing their name.
* **Better pickers:** search by words in any order, with each value's room and reading in the list.
* **Names:** the setting that shortens device names can now shorten room names and Flow names on Flow Buttons too.
* **Unavailable devices** are dimmed with a small **!**, and their old readings don't count in averages.
* **Web dashboards:**
  * A side pane with your dashboards, which can stay open beside them, in your own order.
  * Your home's name and icon in the header, an icon per dashboard, a background colour of your own, and widgets that blend into it.
  * Rotate through dashboards, dim at set times or from a Flow, and go back to a dashboard after a while.
  * Add widget with search and groups, Undo, and an editor that loses no work.
  * Popups can show a camera's live video, full screen.
* **Cameras:** Always live, and battery cameras that refresh up to every 12 hours or only from a Flow.
* **Chart widgets:** small, normal or tall, and the legend above, below or hidden on six more.
* **Flow Buttons:** activate with a double or long press. Widgets try again by themselves after a failed read, and settings are in the same order across all widgets.

Full list: https://github.com/thomassidor/widgetkeeper/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md
