## :test_tube: Widgetkeeper 1.2.0 is in testing

Everything from 1.1.2, and: https://homey.app/a/com.thomassidor.widgetkeeper/test/

* **Nine new chart widgets:** Totals, Scatter (XY), Daily Profile, Min/Max, Ranking, Timeline, Stacked, Lines and Gauge. Totals can also show what your energy cost. Thanks to @pimswj3 for the idea.
* **Charts is now Chart · Basic.** It and Sparklines take any time span up to two years. Widgets already on your dashboards stay as they are.
* **Device names:** show only part of each name, such as the part before `|`, or the name without its room. **Configure → Device names**.
* **Web dashboards:**
  * Screens sign in with a short code or QR code, and each can open on a dashboard of its own.
  * Sync a web dashboard with a Homey dashboard, both ways.
  * A live preview while you edit, copying a widget, and a kiosk mode (`?kiosk=1`).
* **Six more colours:** teal, cyan, indigo, pink, brown and grey.
* **Cameras:** Synology Surveillance Station's cameras, and a setting to turn live view off.
* **Device Quick Actions:** pill tiles and 2 per row. **Sensor Alarms:** 3 per row.
* **Older Homey Pro (2016–2019):** fixes for Sparklines, the chart widgets, Heatmap and the Electricity Overview.

Full list: https://github.com/thomassidor/widgetkeeper/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md
