## :test_tube: Widgetkeeper 1.4.1 is in testing

Everything from 1.4.0, and: https://homey.app/a/com.thomassidor.widgetkeeper/test/

* **New widget, Batteries:** the battery of every device you pick in one tile, lowest first, yellow and red when one needs replacing, at levels you choose. Optionally each cell fills to its level. Thanks to @Drako74 for asking.
  * How it works: https://github.com/thomassidor/widgetkeeper/blob/main/docs/widgets/battery.md
* **Widget Builder: editing tools** in a ribbon, as in Figma or Photoshop: copy and paste (also between designs), select several and align, space or group them, lock and hide, zoom, try a value on your rules, replace a device, and export and import a design.
* **Also in the Widget Builder:** a finer grid, Rectangle, Circle and Line, colours that blend along a value, a median, a device's room in a text, time since a Flow input, a Repeater sorted by value, and device icons.
* **Notes:** format the text with Markdown: **bold**, *italic*, headings and lists.

Full list: https://github.com/thomassidor/widgetkeeper/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md
