## :test_tube: Widgetkeeper 1.3.1 is in testing

Everything from 1.3.0, and: https://homey.app/a/com.thomassidor.widgetkeeper/test/

* **Tile shadows in light mode:** the tiles of transparent widgets (Light Controls, Lights by Room, Device Values, Quick Actions …) now have the soft shadow of Homey's own tiles, so they match them on the dashboard. Prefer them flat? Turn it off under **Configure → Appearance**. Thanks to @saschaeggi for reporting it.
* **Combined Value:** *Rename tiles* gives each device a name of your own, used in the list, the chart and the line under the name.
* **Room Health:** a temperature sensor that reports °F is no longer scored as °C (which made every room red).

Full list: https://github.com/thomassidor/widgetkeeper/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md
