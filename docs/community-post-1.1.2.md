## :test_tube: Widgetkeeper 1.1.2 is in testing

Everything from 1.1.1, and: https://homey.app/a/com.thomassidor.widgetkeeper/test/

* **Charts:** today and yesterday on one chart, each by its time of day, so a day's use (water, energy) compares with the day before. Thanks to @SunBeech for reporting it.
* **Charts and Sparklines:** six new time spans, this and last week (from Monday), month and year. On one chart, this and last week (or month, or year) compare as today and yesterday do. Thanks to @SunBeech for the idea.
* **Charts:** a new *Legend* setting puts each chart's name and units under it. Averages and tapped values keep three digits, so a meter that reports two decimals shows 1,304 W, not 1,304.48 W (Sparklines' highs and lows too).
* **Keypad:** the new *Background* setting's *State colour* fills the widget with the ticked button's colour, so even a small one shows from across the room whether the alarm is armed. Thanks to @Daniel_Fille for the idea.
* **Lights by Room:** *Rename tiles* gives a room and each of its lights a name of your own, as in Light Controls. Thanks to @ChrisG for the idea.

Full list: https://github.com/thomassidor/widgetkeeper/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md
