# Charts widget (`charts`) and the shared chart module

- One chart, two stacked, or two series on one chart (`charts`: `1`/`2`/`overlay`; the user asked for the last, like the Electricity Overview's usage over price) of any logged number, drawn like the Electricity Overview (wk-043, the user's idea). Not transparent: `homey-widget`, with no dark frame of its own.
- **The shared chart module** is `widgets/electricity/public/chart.js` + `chart.css` (Homey serves each widget's folder and has no shared one; Charts loads `../electricity/chart.js`), `window.WidgetkeeperChart`: the path, scale and format helpers (`smoothValues`, `linePath`, `stepPath`, `p015`, `compactNumber` `−1,5k`, `ago` `1h · 30m` …), `attachScrub()` (the pointer, 3 s touch hold and `preventDefault` wiring) and `createKit()`, per widget with its own gradient/clip ids: `axis`, `chip`, `exportSplit`, `midnightLines`, `valueScale` (from zero like the W scales; with `fit`, whole steps just outside the range, a zero line only when it crosses zero, a flat series in the middle; returns `baseY`, where areas and bars close), `dualScale` (`overlay`: left and right scales on one grid, sharing the zero line and steps like the price and usage scales), `trace` (line, gradient area, the split below zero), `bars` ….
  - The class names are the Electricity Overview's (`.ew` is the shared scope), so its markup didn't change: `test/electricity-render.test.ts` compares 19 renders with `test/fixtures/electricity-render.json`, recorded before the move (delete it to re-record, on purpose). Each `widget.css` adds its series' colours and their `.export` variants after `chart.css` (an `.export` rule before a series rule would lose).
  - `chart.css`/`chart.js` load before `widget.*` in both `index.html`s, the dev pages that mount either, `DEPS` in `test/helpers/loadWidget.ts` and `DEPS` in the stack's `mount.js` (once per page); `test/widget-html.test.ts` ('shared files') checks they agree.
- Settings (20): `charts`, `sharedScale` (off; only with `overlay`, below), `showHeader` (on), `legend` (below), then per chart N: `chartN` (autocomplete `app.sparklines.listSlots`, logged numbers), `spanN` (Sparklines' `1h` … `7d`, `today`, `yesterday`, then `thisWeek`, `lastWeek`, `thisMonth`, `lastMonth`, `thisYear`, `lastYear`; default 24 h), `styleN` (`area` default, `line`, `bars`), `colorN` (the Heatmap's six; blue, then green), `rangeN` (`fit` default, `zero`), `negativeN` ("Below zero in yellow", off), `smoothN` (off), `titleN` (else `Device · Capability`). The user chose a per-chart scale and yellow setting, and an optional header. With `overlay`, `span2` and `range2` are ignored (their hints say so): series 2 takes chart 1's span (`mount.js` asks with it only when both charts are picked) and kind of scale. Except two calendar periods of one kind (today with yesterday, this week with last, this month or year with last, either way, or the same one twice; the kit's `spanUnit()`, in the widget; `mount.js` asks `/state` for the widget's `spans()`): series 2 keeps its own, so one capability's today and yesterday (or this month and last) can be compared (wk-102, SunBeech's daily water use; before, yesterday was asked for and drawn as today; widened to weeks, months and years with wk-104).
- **Today and Yesterday** (the user's request, 2026-10-23): a calendar day in the phone's local time (the kit's `timeSpan()`, also Sparklines'; a DST day is 23 or 25 h), `windowOf()` → `{from, to, end, live, unit}`. Today's axis is the whole day, 00–24 (the user's choice over midnight → now), the line, bars (an hour each) and dot up to now, the rest empty. Yesterday ends at midnight: no live values, no value carried on, and the header shows the day's average (`charts.yesterdayAvg` "Yesterday · avg", the user's choice; the mean of the drawn points or bars, which are evenly spaced and carry the last value), a tap still the tapped point. Both label 6:00/12:00/18:00 without Now, are redrawn every minute (so they roll over at midnight) and show `hh:mm` on a tap. The data is Homey's own day (`today`/`yesterday` logs, see Sparklines' note); just after midnight Today shows No history yet until a few minutes are logged and the 5-min refetch brings them.
- **Weeks, months and years** (wk-104, SunBeech's ask; the user added This beside Last, 2026-10-27): the same as Today and Yesterday, a whole local week (from Monday, as Homey's `lastWeek` log is), month or year (`timeSpan()`'s `unit`). This one runs up to now with the value now in the header; the last one ends at its end, its average in the header (`charts.lastWeekAvg`, `lastMonthAvg`, `lastYearAvg`; bars weigh by their length, as months differ). Bars: a day each over a week or a month (24 h on the clock, so a DST day's is 23 or 25 h), a calendar month each over a year (`chartBuckets()`'s `'month'`). x labels: a week the weekday at noon with dashed midnights (as 7 d, but kept from 12 px in, so Monday shows at 300 px), a month the 7th, 14th, 21st and 28th (`7 Sep`), a year the months from their 1st (`text-anchor: start`, so January isn't dropped against the scale), as many as fit 40 px apart (every other at 358 px); no midnights on a month or year, no Now on any. A tap: `Mon 14:00`, a day's bar `Tue 15 Sep`, a month's point `15 Sep 14:00`, a year's `15 Sep`, a month's bar `September 2025`. A month's or year's log is Homey's 6-hour averages (CLAUDE.md, Insights), so live values aren't appended on a month or year (the kit's `sixHourSpan()`, Sparklines too): a meter's would pile up behind the log; the value now still carries the line to now. Their log starts with the 6 hours holding the period's start, so a point of those logged before it counts from the start (`loggedPoints()`). This month and year's log ends at the last finished 6 hours, so that last stretch is a straight line to the value now.
- **Legend** (`legend`, wk-104, SunBeech's ask): `above` (default; a missing setting) or `below`: each chart's chips move under its time labels (`.ch.legend-below`, the chips after the svg, static with 8 px above) and the plot's top grid line from `CHIP_H` (38) to `TOP_PAD` (10, room for the top scale label). The header stays on top.
- No service: `GET /state?charts=<deviceId:capId>~<span>,…` → `{charts, language}`, each Sparklines' `getState([slot], span)`. Live: `values:state`, appended as Sparklines does; redrawn every span/300 (1–60 s); refetched every 5 min.
- Layout: the Electricity Overview's header (a column per chart: the value in the chart's colour, the export colour below zero with `negative`, and `Now` or the tapped time; a tapped point or bar and an average to three significant digits within the capability's decimals, the kit's `formatNumber(…, averaged)`, so the frient meter's 2 decimals give `1,304 W`, not `1,304.48 W`, the user's ask after seeing the real data, 2026-10-27; the value now as Homey reports it), then per chart its chip (title – units) and plot: 122 px for one chart, 83 px each for two (the price and live heights). x labels: up to 6 h relative, 24 h the hours every 6 h, longer the weekday at noon; dashed midnights (calendar spans above). Bars (`chartBuckets()`): on the clock, 5 min (1 h) … 6 h (5 d, 7 d), a day or a month on the calendar spans, the average of their points or the last value before, cut to the span; the value now only fills a last bar without points. A 0–1 `%` shows 0–100 (the kit's `scaled()`). Bars of whole hours stay on the clock's hours across a DST change (that day's is an hour longer or shorter). Axis thousands have the decimals the value needs (`1,25k` on a 250 step). Each chart scrubs on its own; the tapped point is kept as a time (`selectedIndex()` finds it on each render, so it stays put as the window moves) and dropped once it leaves the window.
- `overlay`: one box and one 122 px plot, both chips in its chip row (`data-color`, shortened to fit), chart 1's scale on the left, chart 2's on the right (always both, even with the same units: the user's choice; `R` = 34 px), `kit.dualScale()`. With `sharedScale` ("One scale for both", Stefan Schweiger's PR #14, 2026-10-26, in 1.1.1, so two values compare directly), one `kit.valueScale()` on the left over both series' drawn values (chart 1's kind of scale; a series that can't be drawn leaves it to the other), and the plot as wide as one chart's (no `R`). Stacked charts ignore it. Each series draws into its own `<g data-color>` (`drawSeries()`) with its own x from its own window (the axis, labels and midnights are chart 1's; yesterday under today is spread over the width by its time of day, so a 23 or 25 h DST day is up to an hour off by its end, as last month under this one is by its place in the month: a 30-day September is spread over October's 31, so up to a day off by its end), series 2 on top, then both dots; two bar series share each slot half and half. A tap selects both series at that x. A series that can't be drawn leaves the other; the note shows only when neither can.
- The preview frames `chart-dark`/`chart-light` are the simple version the user asked for: one purple chart with its title chip (wk-063: the dot, the title as the other previews' title pill, the muted units, on the widget's chip colour) and no header (purple because blue and green read as the Electricity Overview). The README screenshot `charts` is inline in `mock-dashboard.js`. Not yet tried on the Homey or the phones.
