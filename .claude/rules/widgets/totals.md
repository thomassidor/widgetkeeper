# Chart · Totals widget (`totals`, called Totals per Period while it was built; renamed 2026-10-30, as the other chart widgets, by what it offers)

- How much a value that only counts up went up per hour, day or month, like the Homey app's Energy tab: a meter's kWh or m³, a rain gauge's mm, hours of TV (wk-099, pimswj3 on the forum, 2026-10-27: bars per week, month and year, "hour nice to have", the current and previous period). Named "Usage Chart" at first; the user renamed it the same day (it isn't only for meters), with each language's word for a chart first (`Graf · …`, `Diagramm · …`) so it sits beside Charts in Homey's widget list. Its own widget rather than a Charts mode: the user's rule (2026-10-27) is that a mode bringing settings of its own becomes its own chart widget on the shared chart module, while what applies to every series (a time span, a colour) goes into Charts, whose flat settings list was getting crowded. Not transparent: `homey-widget`. Charts' one-chart layout (header, chip row, 122 px plot, the `ew` classes and `chart.css`), drawn by the chart module (`../electricity/chart.js`, see Charts' note).
- Settings (9): `slot` ("Value that counts up", autocomplete `app.sparklines.listSlots`, every logged number; the hint names meters, rain and TV hours), `count`, `span` and `leaveOut` (the catalog's time span block on its own rows, 1.2.0: any number of a unit, rolling or whole periods, as Chart · History's; default 1 week from Monday, this week; the widget's `period` option is the span string, the kit's `spanSetting()`), the settings from `scripts/chart-settings.json` (`npm run chart-settings`), `compare` ("Compare with the period before", off), `title` (else `Device · Capability`), `showHeader` ("Show the totals at the top", on), `color` (the Heatmap's six, blue; the kit's `data-wk-color`, its bright set), `colorStyle` (Vivid: bars at 90 %, the period before at 45 %).
- `GET /state?charts=<slot>~<period>~<from>~<end>~<unit>~use[,<the period before's>]` → `{charts: [current, previous?], language}` (1.2.0, Charts' `lib/chartState.ts`): the period's window and with compare the one before's (`windows()`, the kit's `timeSpan(period, now, 1)`), each the value's running total from Sparklines' `getWindows()` (the logs `planLogs()` picks: the period before reads what `PREV_LOGS` named, e.g. the month before last `last3Months`). The value's autocomplete lists meters first (`listSlots(query, 'counter')`). No service of its own. Live: `values:state`, through `C.meterTotal()` onto the running total; redrawn every minute (the live bar, the period rolling over); refetched every 5 min.
- **The use per bar** (`C.useBuckets()`): the service sends the value's running total (`runningTotal()` in `lib/series.ts`: a value under half the highest since the last reset is a reset and the total counts on; a smaller dip holds; an Insights step averaged across a reset, the one before a lower one, is left out) at each log entry's middle (`meterPoints()`, ≤ 400 points), and each bar is the rise of the line through those points between its edges, on the phone's local clock (`C.buckets()`' steps, Charts' own since 1.1.2: an hour, a day as midnight to midnight, a calendar month). Past the last point the line goes on at its last rate for a step and a half, then holds: Insights leaves out a closed period's last 6-hour step when it straddles local midnight (`lastMonth` ended at 30 Sep 12:00Z), and doesn't log an unchanged value. The live bar ends at the total now. Months and years come from 6-hour logs, so a day's edges in a month can be off by an hour or two of use; `last2Years` (the year before last, for `lastYear` with compare) is weekly.
- Compare (unlike Charts' two-on-one-chart this-against-last, a line or averages, from 1.1.2): the period before (`timeSpan(period, now, 1)`) in each slot's left half, this period's in the right, paired by index (day 3 with day 3, March with March; a 31st with no partner has none), `.series-bars.prev` at 30 % of the colour; its chip names it (Yesterday, Last week …, or for a closed period Day before yesterday, Week before last …). The header gets a second, muted column: the period before's total, or the tapped slot's bar.
- Header: the period's total (up to two decimals under 10, one under 1000, none above, at most the capability's), sub the period's name (one whole day, week, month or year: Today, Last week, Month before last …; any other span its dates, `1 Jul – 30 Sep`, `windowName()`); bars from the chart module's `barUnit()` (an hour, a day, a month; for other spans 12–28 on the clock), x labels for one whole week, month or year as below, else the chart kit's `timeLabels()`; a tapped slot shows its use and `13:00–14:00`, the day (`Intl` `{weekday: 'short', day: 'numeric'}` in Homey's language: `27 Tue`, Danish `tir. den 27.`) or the month. Tapped slot kept as its start time, dropped once the period moves on. x labels: a day 6:00/12:00/18:00, else `C.calendarLabels()` (weekdays, days 1·8·15·22·29, months; narrow below 34 px a day or 30 px a month: at a phone's width the year is J F M …, like the Energy tab).
- Messages: the kit's `messageLine()`: a lasting one (no value picked, a failed first load) replaces the chart, a passing one (a failed refresh) sits under it for 8 s.
- `dev/totals-preview.html` (`mock-totals.js`: the showcase's "Main meter", a day/night and winter profile), the preview frames `tt-dark`/`tt-light` (a week of orange bar pairs: blue and green read as the Electricity Overview, purple as Charts), the README screenshot `totals` (this year against last year on the showcase's clock). `npm run test-dashboards -- totals`: every period, six with compare, a second value (a meter, else anything logged) without totals, a deleted device, nothing picked, one wide. Not yet tried on the Homey or the phones.
