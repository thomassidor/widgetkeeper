# Chart · X against Y widget (`scatter`)

- One value against another over a time span: each dot is where both were at the same time (their averages over a dot's time, `C.alignSeries()`: both on one clock grid, the last value carried over gaps), older dots fainter, the newest ringed, and the least-squares trend line (`C.trendLine()`) dashed over them, its slope at the top. The user's own example of a new chart type (1.2.0, mocked up with five others; the user picked it, Typical Day, Daily Range and Compare). Not transparent: `homey-widget`. Built on the chart frame (`C.createFrame`, Charts' note) and the mount helper (`C.wireChart`); its own plot: 160 px tall (dots need the height), a left value scale (`valueScale`, always fitted) and a bottom one (`kit.xValueScale()`, labels at least 40 px apart), the x value's chip under its labels at the right with a neutral dot, the y value's (or the title) on top.
- Settings (12, from `scripts/chart-settings.json`, `npm run chart-settings`): `xValue` ("Value across (x)") and `yValue` ("Value up (y)"), both Sparklines' `listSlots` (logged numbers); the time span block (`count`, `span`, `leaveOut` on their own rows; default 7 days, the kit's `spanSetting()`); `dots` ("One dot per": `auto` default, `hour`, `day`); `trend` ("Trend line", on); `fade` ("Fade older dots", on); `title` (else the y value's `Device · Capability`); `showHeader` (Charts' title, its own hint); `color` (blue; the kit's `data-wk-color`, its bright set); `colorStyle` (Vivid: each dot a rim, older ones still fainter).
- A dot's time with `auto` (`dotUnit()`): the shortest of 1 min, 5 min, 15 min, 1 h, 3 h, 6 h, a day, a week giving at most 400 dots, never under 6 h where Homey's log is 6-hourly (the kit's `sixHourSpan()`): 24 h is 5 min (288), 7 days an hour (168), 30 days 6 h.
- Header: the newest dot's (or the tapped one's) y value in the widget's colour, its x value and `Now` (or the dot's time, `14:00–15:00`, a weekday before it over several days, a day's date) under it; with the trend a second column, the slope (`maximumSignificantDigits: 3`, a real minus) in `<y units>/<x units>` and `Trend`, in the text colour. Without both values or without two dots, one column. A tap picks the nearest dot within 24 px in 2D (the frame's `scrub` passes the pointer's x and y), kept as its time and dropped once it's left the span.
- `GET /state?charts=<x>~<span>~<from>~<end>~<unit>,<y>~…` → `{charts: [x, y], language}` (Charts' `lib/chartState.ts`; `windows()`, one window for both). Live: `values:state` onto the value it is (not on 6-hourly spans), redrawn every minute, refetched every 5 min.
- Messages: nothing picked says `selectValues`; a missing value's chip says Unavailable (the note only when the title stands in the value up's chip); both there but fewer than two dots: `noHistory`.
- `dev/scatter-preview.html` (`mock-scatter.js`: the showcase house's outdoor temperature and heat pump, never real readings), the preview frames `xy-dark`/`xy-light` (a falling cloud of red dots: blue and green read as the Electricity Overview, purple as Charts, orange as Totals per Period), the README screenshot `scatter` (a week, on the showcase's clock). `npm run test-dashboards -- scatter` (under Test charts): defaults, 24 h, 30 days a dot a day, this month without trend or fading in Vivid, last week with a title and no header, 6 h a dot an hour, two temperatures, a deleted device, nothing picked. Not yet tried on the Homey or the phones.
