# Combined Value widget (`combined`)

- One full-width tile: one number capability read from several devices, folded into one value. The user's pick of two rounds of mock-ups (2026-11-04; `temp/aggregate-mockup.html`, then `temp/aggregate-mockup-2.html` with the decisions): Device Values' compact tile, full width, with the value large on the right and the range under the name (no device count); the dots and the share bar of the first round, generalized, on by default; the pill is Device Values' pill with the dots on the right; the range optional everywhere. The name "Combined Value" and the id were picked when building it (the user didn't object to the working name). A tap opens every device's value under the tile (below; 1.3.0, the user's pick of improvements, 2026-11-04, with the OK range, the count, Median and the unit check). Transparent.
- Settings: `capability` (autocomplete, Value Matrix's `listColumns()`: every number capability on the Homey by id, `units · N devices`, `None` first), `show` (`avg` default, `median`, `sum`, `min`, `max`, `range` "Lowest – highest", `out` "Number outside the OK range"), `name` (text; empty: the capability's title from the first counted device), `okRange` ("OK range", text, below), `showRange` (on; "Show the range", the id of Chart · Ranking's and Chart · Gauge's: `range` until the settings review, 2026-11-04, where every chart's `range` is its Scale), `chart` (on), `tileStyle` (Device Values' `compact`/`pill`, its hint), `color` (`default`, a plain tile, then the palette; not in `PICKERS`, whose colour defaults to blue), `colorStyle`. The `devices` setting has no filter. An autocomplete doesn't get the devices, so the value is picked across the Homey and the devices narrow it.
- `lib/CombinedService.ts` only picks the devices: the picked ones that have the capability (`matrixCaps()`), in order, a repeat once (a deleted one is `{id, missing: true}`, logged; one without the value isn't counted at all); with none picked, every device with it, by name (`devicesAndZones()`, its shared 5 s read). Units: the value's are those most counted devices report it in (the first's on a tie, `describeCapability()`); a device in others comes back with `otherUnits` (its units, `''` for none) and is logged once while the set stays the same. `describe()` is Diagnostics' `combined`: per value and devices picked (the last 10) its last request, `{at, picked, counted, notFound, units, otherUnits: [{id, units}]}`. The readings are Value Matrix's cells (`ValueMatrixService.getState(ids, [capabilityId])`): its tracking, device names (`shownName()`) and realtime `valuematrix:state`, which the mount filters by the counted ids and the capability. No service of its own to stop.
- `GET /state?capabilityId=&deviceIds=` (`deviceIds` optional) → `{capability: {id, title, units, decimals, min, max} | null, devices: [{id, name, value: number | null, otherUnits?: string} | {id, missing: true}]}`; `capability` null when no counted device has it (the widget says `noDevices`). Without a capability (or `None`) the mount says `selectValue` and asks nothing. The kit's `wireDeviceState()` with `devicesOptional` and `event: null` (the realtime filter is its own, below): refetched every 5 min (also picks up a new device when none were picked) and on a device names change.
- Folding (`combineValues()`, on `window`): the readings that are finite numbers; a missing device, a null reading or one in `otherUnits` is left out and the line says `N of M not read` in place of the range (`N of M left out` when one is in other units; a dash for the value when none answered). Median: the middle reading, the average of the middle two for an even count, with `averaged` like the average. Average and sum as Homey reports (a 0–1 `%` is scaled in `formatNumber()`); the average with `averaged` (three significant digits within the decimals). Numbers in the device locale, `−` for minus; units tight for `°…`/`%`, else spaced; on the tile the units are a 20 medium span after the 32 bold number (`.cv-number`, `.cv-units`).
- The line under the name: Lowest/Highest put the device first (without the chart the only place it shows), then with the range on only its other end (`Bedroom · up to 23.1°C`, `Office · from 19.6°C`: the mock-ups' repeat of the value as the range's end, fixed at the user's ask, 2026-11-04); the others the range (`19.6–23.1°C`; spaced ` – ` when a number is negative); Lowest – highest is the value itself, so no line and the name centred (`.cv-head.one`). The pill puts the value first on that line (`21.8°C · 19.6–23.1°C`) and hides `.cv-value`.
- OK range (`okRange`): the kit's `okRange()`/`okLevel()` (Value Matrix's syntax and levels, moved to the kit for this: `18–24`, `<1000`, `>20`; `out` past it, `far` half its width, or half the limit, past it), on the shown numbers (`scaled()`, so a 0–1 `%` is in percent). **Empty is none**, unlike Value Matrix: the devices may be an outdoor sensor or a freezer, which its usual indoor ranges coloured red (seen in the preview). The tile takes the shown value's level (Lowest – highest the worse end; the count the worst device) as `data-level` `out`/`far` only (inside is left plain): `--wk-orange`/`--wk-red` mixed 22 % into the tile in Muted, over the `color` setting's tint; Vivid fills it with the level's colour and `--wk-on-*` text, dots and track as a Vivid colour does. A dot outside it is `--wk-orange-bright`/`--wk-red-bright` (the text colour on a filled tile).
- Number outside the OK range (`out`): the count large with `of N` (`ofTotal`, a space wide, `.cv-units.count`) in place of the units; the line names those outside, worst first (`Attic, Hall`), or `All within range`; without a range a dash and `Set an OK range in the settings`. The dots as Average's without the tick.
- The list (`.cv-list`, under the tile in both styles, the tile's look; `cv-open` on the root): the kit's `onTap()` on the tile (`role="button"`, `aria-expanded`, keys) opens it, a tap on either closes it; the mount's `onTap` gives `hapticFeedback()`. A row per reading (an 8 px mark, the name, the value in 500): highest first for Sum and Highest, worst first then lowest for the count, else lowest first; the device Lowest/Highest comes from bold; a mark orange/red outside the OK range. Then the unread in muted (`–`, or the value in its own units, titled `other unit, left out`) and `N unavailable`. Re-rendered with every change while open; `toggle()` opens it from a test or the preview.
- The chart (`data-chart` on the root: `dots`, `share`, `none`): a sum of values all zero or more (and above zero in total) gets the share bar, everything else the dots.
  - Dots: one per device on a shared scale (`scaleOf()`: 0–100 for `%`, so a low battery sits left; else the lowest to the highest with 8 % room, kept from going below zero when every value is above it), the band lowest to highest (30 % accent, 45 % in Vivid), a zero mark where the scale crosses it, the average a 3 px accent tick (Average only). Lowest/Highest highlight that device's dot, Lowest – highest both ends and the band. Under the strip the lowest and highest devices' names (the one the value comes from in the text colour, 500); one device, one name. Each dot's `title` is `name: value`.
  - Share: every device with four or fewer, else the biggest three and Others; kit.css's bright blue, green, orange, purple and the dots' grey for Others, mixed 85 % into the tile in Muted, full in Vivid; the legend names each with its rounded %. The pill has no legend and no end names.
- Look: Device Values' tile (`#181920` dark with the rim, 10 px radius, 8/10 padding), the head 40 px (the value's line), the icon the kit's `valueGlyph()` (moved there from Device Values; the capability's own icon isn't read, as Value Matrix's cells don't carry it). The pill: Sensor Alarms' 52 px, `#2B2C36` dark, the 24 px muted icon, 14/18 lines, the chart 96 px on the right. Track and dot colours are mixed from the text colour into the tile's own background (`--cv-bg`), so they work on a tinted or a Vivid tile.
- Colours: tinted (CLAUDE.md *Widget styling*). A colour mixes `--wk-c` 22 % into the tile (Muted) and makes the accent its bright set; Vivid fills the tile with the shade and draws text, dots and share in `--wk-on` (the share's parts by strength). Vivid without a colour only strengthens the share and the band.
- `dev/combined-preview.html` (`?mode=light`, `?w=300`: seventeen cases, each tile then pill; two with the list open). README screenshot `combined` (three widgets stacked: the rooms' average, the big users' power, the lowest battery as a pill; made up in `dev/mock-dashboard.js`). Preview frames `cv-dark`/`cv-light`. `npm run test-dashboards -- combined`. A Smart Stack page type. Not yet seen on the Homey or in the phone apps.
- Green when fine (`greenWhenFine`, after `okRange`, off; Room Health's setting and strings; the settings review, 2026-11-04): on, a tile whose shown value is inside the OK range takes `data-level="good"`, green as `out`/`far` take theirs (22 % in Muted, the green shade with `--wk-on-green` in Vivid). Without an OK range nothing is judged.
- An unavailable device (CLAUDE.md *Unavailable devices*): `CombinedService` answers it as `{id, missing: true}`, so it's left out and the line says `N of M not read`, the list `N unavailable`.
- Rename tiles (`renameTiles`, first, off; 1.3.1, a user's request relayed by the user, 2026-11-14; see `tile-names.md`): keyed by the device id. A typed name stands for the device everywhere the widget names one (the line's Lowest/Highest device and the names outside the OK range, the dots' end names and titles, the share legend, the list), as each device's `name` is swapped before folding (`own` keeps the device's for the field's placeholder). In rename mode the list stays open and taps do nothing; `renderRenameList()` is a row per answering device (also one without a reading or in other units, not a missing one) in the order picked, its rows kept in `renameRows` and only moved where out of place, so a realtime change doesn't take a field's focus. The field replaces the row's name (`.cv.renaming .cv-row-name`). A failed save shakes the tile and says `renameFailed`. Settings hint names the list, not the tiles. `dev/rename-preview.html` has it (Lowest, a typed name, a missing device); `npm run test-dashboards -- combined` a Rename tiles widget with a name typed.
