# Chart · Gauge widget (`gauge`)

- One value now on a dial: a 240° arc (`SWEEP` 120° each way from the top), the value's arc from the scale's start with a ringed knob at its end, the value in the middle (32 bold, its units 17 muted, tight before °/%), and with `showRange` the time span's lowest to highest (with the value now) as a thin band inside the track and as `Range 430–1,110` under the value; the scale's ends under the arc's. The user's pick (2026-10-31) of the chart types missing after 1.2.0's (a gauge, a share of a whole, a histogram, a calendar heatmap). Built from Chart · Ranking's code (one value now and its range over a span). Not transparent: `homey-widget`. On the chart frame and mount helper (Charts' note), without its header (`showHeader: false`; the value is in the dial); the chip row (title, else `Device · Capability`, with its units, and a `Range` chip) over the svg's top, so the dial starts `CHIP_H` (36) down. Radius at most 80 px (`MAX_R`), 12 px track.
- Data: Charts' `/state` (`lib/chartState.ts`), one window (`windows()`), Sparklines' `listSlots` (logged numbers; `VALUE_SETTINGS.gauge`). Live: `values:state` onto the value (the frame's `pushLive()`); redrawn every minute; refetched every 5 min.
- Scale (`scale`): `auto` (default): 0–100 for a `%` (a 0–1 one scaled, the kit's `scaled()`), else the chart kit's `ticks({fit: true})` around the span's values, the value now and, with zones, both limits; `custom`: `scaleMin`–`scaleMax` (0–100 by default), ignored when `max <= min`. A value past the scale fills the arc to its end. Not the capability's own min/max (a temperature's driver range would be far too wide).
- Levels (`zones`, "Colour by level"): `none` (default; the value in the widget's colour), `rising` ("Higher is worse": green, yellow from `yellowAt`, red from `redAt`; defaults 1000 and 1400, CO₂ in ppm) or `falling` ("Lower is worse": yellow at or below `yellowAt`, red at or below `redAt`). `zoneOf()` (also on `createGaugeWidget.zoneOf`, for the test). The track becomes a butt-ended stretch per level, cut at the limits inside the scale, mixed with the card (`--gg-zone-mix` 28 %, opaque, so the round ends, drawn as dots in their level's colour, don't double up); the value's arc, its knob, the number and the chip's dot take the value's level colour. The palette's bright green, yellow and red (`--wk-*-bright`).
- Settings (13, from `scripts/chart-settings.json`): `slot`, `scale`, `  – From`, `  – To`, `zones`, `  – Yellow at`, `  – Red at`, the time span block without Leave out (`count` 1, `span` `calDays`: today; hint `count.gauge`, for the range), `showRange`, `title`, `color` (blue), `colorStyle` (hint `colorStyle.gauge`). The number settings arrive as numbers, or as strings from a web dashboard's editor; the mount reads both, an empty one as missing.
- Vivid: the levels at 50 %, the track without levels in the widget's colour (22 %), and without levels the range band in the widget's colour.
- `dev/gauge-preview.html` (`mock-gauge.js`: the showcase house's office CO₂ through a working day, the kitchen's humidity and the front door's battery; never real readings), the preview frames `gg-dark`/`gg-light` (green, yellow and red stretches, the value green), the README screenshot `gauge` (the office's CO₂ with levels). Previews and the screenshot rendered in the cloud session (Playwright's `chrome-headless-shell` as `EDGE`, Roboto in `~/.fonts`, renders.md); a re-render of Lines' dark preview there was byte-identical, its screenshot wasn't, so that one was left as committed. `npm run test-dashboards -- gauge` (under Test charts). Not yet tried on the Homey or the phones.
