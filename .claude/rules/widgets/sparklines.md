# Sparklines widget (`sparklines`)

- Device Values' tiles, 2 or 1 per row (`columns`, `"2"`), 104 px: the capability icon and value on top, a 36 px chart, the device name below. A 1.5 px line in Homey's blue with a 15 % area and a dot on the latest point; the highest (`↑23`) and lowest (`↓19.5`) shown value at its right edge, 12/16 muted, without units (the user chose this over a footer line or the name row), to three significant digits within the capability's decimals (the kit's `formatNumber(…, averaged)`: the points are averages, so a 2-decimal meter shows `↑12,273`, not `↑12,273.24`; the value on top as Homey reports it). Display only; transparent. `lib/SparklineService.ts`.
- Settings (from the chart catalog since 1.2.0, `scripts/chart-settings.json`'s `sparklines`; change them there and run `npm run chart-settings`): `renameTiles`, the time span block `count` (24), `span` (the unit, `hours`) and `leaveOut`, as Chart · Basic's (Charts' note, *Time spans*; the user's ask, 2026-10-31: Sparklines was the last with the fixed list), `columns`, `colorStyle`, `slot1`…`slot6` (as Device Values', listing only numbers with `insights === true`, `sparkCaps()`, or every number on an older Homey Pro, whose capabilities have no `insights`: Heatmap's note). Booleans were left out: their log is only the last 50 changes.
- History: Insights `lastHour` (5 s steps), `last6Hours` (1 min), `last24Hours` (5 min), `last3Days` (1 h; also for `2d`) or `last7Days` (1 h; also for `5d`); `last2Days` and `last5Days` are `Invalid Resolution` (2026-10-06/09). `today` and `yesterday` (2026-10-23) are 5-min logs over the Homey's own calendar day: `start` its midnight, `end` the next 5-min mark after now (`today`) or a step past the next midnight (`yesterday`); `calendarWindow()` downsamples over that, up to now. The weeks, months and years (wk-104, 2026-10-27; the same `calendar` flag in `SPANS`) are Homey's `thisWeek`/`lastWeek` (1 h, from Monday), `thisMonth`/`lastMonth` and `thisYear`/`lastYear` (6 h, from the UTC 6 hours holding the local start; this month's and year's end at the last finished 6 hours). The widget draws the phone's local period (the kit's `timeSpan()`): today 00–24 (this week, month, year: the whole period) with the line up to now, yesterday (the last week, month, year) up to its end with no live points and no value carried on (the tile's value on top stays the value now). `downsample()` averages into at most 120 points at the bucket middles, no bucket shorter than the log's step (`2d` is 48 hourly points, not stairs; `bucketAverage` carries the last value over gaps). Cached per slot and span for 5 min with shared fetches (a finished day, week, month or year until the period after it could end, `historyTtl()` with `SPANS`' `finished`, once a step has passed since its log's `end`), dropped after 10 min without a request; the raw log is shared per slot and resolution for 5 min (`readLog()`), so 2d/3d and 5d/7d read it once. A failed log gives no history (cached too: 5 min, or 30 s when Homey was busy) but still the value. Read through `readCapabilityLog()` (until 2026-10-09 every power sparkline started empty: a user's report).
- **For Chart · Totals** (wk-099): its running totals (`use`) and the period before come through the chart windows below (`getWindows()`); the `prev`/`use` options `getState()` had for it, `PREV_LOGS` and `meterPoints()` went in the 1.2.0 code review, unused once Totals asked by window. `use` gives a running total (`runningTotal()` in `lib/series.ts`: a value under half the highest since the last reset is a reset and counts on; a smaller dip holds), each sample at its own log's step middle (`meterPointsOf()`, runs averaged to ≤ 400 points), with `meter: {offset, high}` for live values.
- **Chart windows** (1.2.0, `getWindows(requests)`, for every chart widget through Charts' `lib/chartState.ts`): each request is a window `[from, end]` the widget worked out on the phone's clock, with its span's calendar `unit` and `use`. `planLogs()` (`lib/logPlan.ts`, `INSIGHTS_LOGS`: each log's step and the window it holds at now in the Homey's time zone, `periodStart()`) picks the logs: a calendar window its unit's (today `today`, last week `lastWeek`, three months from the 1st `thisMonth` + `last3Months`), a rolling one the rolling logs, then the others where those can't hold it (the day before yesterday `last3Days`, a rolling half year `thisYear` + `lastYear`); of the sets of up to three that hold it the finest coarsest step, then the fewest logs (each is a request), then the least read; older than two years, what there is. So each old span reads the log it read before (`test/log-plan.test.ts`). `mergeLogs()` stitches them (the finer log where they overlap), then `downsampleSamples()` over the window up to where the logs end (≤ 120, no bucket under the coarsest step; this month's and year's logs end up to 12 h before now, and buckets past that would carry the last value on as if logged) or `meterPointsOf()` (each sample at its own log's step middle). Each history comes with `step`, the coarsest log step read, which the widgets' `sixHourLog()` reads (`getState()` sends its span's). The plan and each log's `ttl` are worked out once per distinct window in a request (`histories()`, which Chart · Timeline calls directly, having read the values' state itself). Raw logs are cached per slot and resolution with their own `ttl` (`logTtl()`: a finished period's until the next begins, once its last step has passed; a failed read 5 min at most, a busy Homey's 30 s) and dropped 10 min after the last request; a window's history isn't cached beyond them. `listSlots(query, 'counter')` lists `meter_*` first (`listCapabilitySlots()`' `first`).
- **Apps' logged numbers** (1.2.0, a user's ask, 2026-11-03: they copied each Better Logic variable into a Device Capabilities device to chart it): every value setting in `VALUE_SETTINGS` but a booleans-only one also lists the number logs apps keep themselves (`lib/appLogs.ts`, `listLogSlots()`), after the devices' values, as `App · Title` (the units as the description; Better Logic's `Value` isn't units, `logUnits()`), id `log:<appId>:<the log id's rest, base64url>` (`logSlot()`/`parseLogSlot()`: a variable's name is the log id's end as typed, and base64url keeps `,` `~` `:` `%` out of the chart requests). Homey's own Logic variables have logs too (CLAUDE.md, Insights) but are left out (the user's choice, 2026-11-03). `ValueService.getState()` answers such a slot itself (`logState()`): `deviceId` `log:<appId>` and `capabilityId` the encoded name (so `<deviceId>:<capabilityId>` is the slot again), the app as `name` (`apps.getApps()`, as the log's `ownerName` is null), the log's title, units and decimals as the capability (no icon: the gauge glyph), and the log list's `lastValue` (`insights.getLogs()`, shared a minute) as the value; `{missing}` once the log is gone (a deleted variable) or isn't a number. `readLog()` reads it by its own `uri` and full id. No realtime: the value and line move on the 5-min refetch. Seen on the Homey (2026-11-03): Better Logic 4.2.3's `Test` in Chart · Basic's `/state` and Homey's own autocomplete. Not yet drawn on a phone; Sparklines' tile names it by the app, so several variables want Rename tiles.
- Live: `ValueService.getState()` gives the name, icon and value, so tracking and `values:state` are Device Values'. Each live number is appended (not on a month or year, the kit's `sixHourLog()`, by the `step` `/state` sends with each history: a month's or year's 6-hour log would have a meter's values piling up behind it) and the line runs on to now at the current value; a refresh keeps the live points newer than its last point. Redrawn every `span / 120` (10–60 s), so the line scrolls; not while hidden or off screen in a Smart Stack (`setVisible`: live values are kept and drawn when it's back).
- `GET /state?slots=a:cap,b:cap&window=<span>~<from>~<end>~<unit>`: Device Values' entries plus `points: [t, v][]`. The mount turns the settings into a span with the kit's `spanSetting()` (a span stored before 1.2.0, `24h`, `today` …, stays until a unit is picked) and the widget's `windowQuery()` gives the window it draws, which `api.ts` reads as the chart widgets' (`chartStates()`, `getWindows()`, below). A page loaded before 1.2.0 still sends `span=24h`, answered by `getState()` and `SPANS` above.
- It copies Device Values' glyphs (trimmed to numbers; the number formatting is the kit's); `sparkPath()` (on `window`) draws the paths. The SVG elements stay in place and are measured at each render (a `ResizeObserver` redraws).
- Colour style (`colorStyle`, Muted/Vivid; CLAUDE.md *Widget styling*, `kit.css`): after `columns`. Vivid: the area at 0.45 and a 2 px line; the line keeps its colour.
- `columns` lists `1` before `2` since the settings review (2026-11-04, in `scripts/chart-settings.json`: every Tiles per row lists its options from the fewest; ids and the default unchanged).
- `color` (the catalog's, top-level, after `columns`, blue; the settings review's second batch, 2026-11-05): `.sl[data-wk-color]` sets `--sl-accent` to `--wk-c-bright`, the same blue as before for blue; a widget without it (placed before the setting) gets no `data-wk-color` and Homey's blue.
- `smooth` ("Smooth chart lines", after `columns`, off; 2026-11-05): `sparkPath(…, smooth)` draws the kit's `smoothValues()`; the y scale, `↑`, `↓` and the value on top stay real, and the ends (so the dot) stay put. The function moved to the kit from the chart module, as Sparklines doesn't load it.
