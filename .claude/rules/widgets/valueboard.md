# Value Board widget (`valueboard`)

- Many values in one widget (a forum ask, 2026-11-03: Device Values with 12 or 24 tiles instead of four 6-tile widgets). Rather than 24 `Tile N: value` slots, one device picker, like Room Health: every value of the picked devices, grouped by room or by device, each group's name on top. The user's pick (2026-11-04) of five directions and three denser ones mocked up in `temp/many-values-directions.html`: A (rooms, a grid of values) as the default layout and F2 (a line of values per room, no names) as Compact, both with A's group names on top, groups by room or device, and optional green on fine. The name and id are a working choice, unreleased. `lib/ValueBoardService.ts`; transparent, Room Health's tile and hairlines. Not a Smart Stack page (tall, like Lights by Room); on the web dashboards through `WEB_APIS`.
- Settings: `renameTiles` (first; below), `layout` (`tiles` default, `compact`), `groupBy` (`rooms` default, `devices`), `values` (`readings` default: `measure_*`, `meter_*` and `alarm_*` by base id, `isReading()`; `all`: everything Device Values' `valueCaps()` shows, so switches, dim, set temperatures, enums and strings), `columns` (Tiles per row, 2/3/4, `3`; Tiles only), `greenWhenFine` (off), `colorStyle`. No device filter.
- Rename tiles (`.claude/rules/tile-names.md`; the settings review, 2026-11-04: Room Health and Value Matrix had it): keys `zone:<id>` for a room (a device without a known room, and every group by device, its id) and the device id for a device, so a device's name is shared by both groupings. The names show in the group headings, the cells' device names and Compact's tapped name. Renaming draws no values: a tile per group with its field, and by room each of its devices' fields under it (indented, a device once); a missing device has none. `render()` waits while a field has the focus (it rebuilds the body) and a realtime value doesn't redraw; a failed save shakes its row (`shaking`, kept for the redraw the message makes).
- `GET /state?deviceIds=&values=all` → `{id, name, zone, values: [{capabilityId, title, type, units, decimals, min, max, values, value}]}` (the device's own order) or `{id, missing}`. Tracked per device (every `valueCaps()` capability, so switching `values` needs no re-track; re-tracked when the capabilities or their types change); realtime `valueboard:state {deviceId, capabilityId, value}`. Names through `deviceNamer()`.
- Front end (`valueBoardGroups()`): rooms by zone in the order first picked (`zone:<id>`; no known zone, a group of its own by device id), or a group per device; each device's values in turn, sorted by `rank()`: temperature, humidity, other measures, meters, alarms, the rest, the battery last. A missing device is a group of its own, `Unavailable`; a device with no values is left out (all of them: `noValues`). Icons: the kit's `valueGlyph()` (moved from Device Values; never the capability's own icon). Booleans in their own words: a contact Open/Closed, motion Motion/No motion, other alarms Alarm/OK, else On/Off.
- Levels (`valueBoardLevel()`; only what has a sense of its own: a temperature may be a fridge's or the garden's): a battery under 10 % `alert`, under 20 % `warn` (Device Values' defaults); an alarm on `alert` (a contact open `warn`, motion never judged), off `fine`; CO₂ (ppm), PM2.5/PM10 (μg/m³), radon (Bq/m³), VOC (ppb) above Room Health's limits `warn`, from 1.5 × `alert`. `fine` is only shown with `greenWhenFine`. Muted: the value (and icon) in the 4.5:1 text colour on a faint wash (14/16/10 %); Vivid: a solid fill of the kit's shade with `--wk-on-*`.
- Tiles: a tile per group; its name (14 medium), then a 2/3/4-column grid with Room Health's hairlines; a cell is the 16 px icon and the value (17 bold; a unit other than °/% small and muted after it), over the device's name (grouped by room) or the value's title (by device), 12/16 muted. Compact: one tile, a group per part with a line between, the name muted on top and the values (14 bold, 14 px icon) running on; a tap (`onTap()`) names the value in the group's heading (`Device · Title`, or the title by device) for 4 s, the others at 45 %; the same tap again puts it back.
- `dev/valueboard-preview.html` (both layouts, green, Vivid, light, by device, 300 and 420 px; `?named=<deviceId>:<capabilityId>` taps one). Not yet seen on the Homey or in the phone apps; `npm run test-dashboards -- valueboard`, every setting's values (the settings review's second batch, 2026-11-05).
