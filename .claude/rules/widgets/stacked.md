# Chart · Stacked widget (`stacked`)

- Who uses the power? Up to six values stacked on one time axis, and with a total the rest as Other (the total less the layers, never below zero), grey at the bottom of the stack. Charts' one-chart plot (122 px) with a left value scale from zero (`valueScale`, not fitted: stacking needs zero). One of the two chart types mocked up with 1.2.0's four and kept for later (`docs/specs/chart-timeline-and-stacked.md`), built at the user's ask (2026-10-29). Not transparent: `homey-widget`. On the chart frame and mount helper (Charts' note).
- **Below zero** (the user's choice, 2026-10-29, over leaving them out): a layer's negative part stacks downwards from the zero line, the positive parts upwards, so a solar inverter and a heat pump never cancel out. Each row's bands (`[lo, hi]` per layer) come from the sums before it on its side; an area per layer and side, its outer edge drawn as its line. Other is counted from the net sum (the meter reads net), so it stays right with solar.
- Data: each value's window through `/state` (`lib/chartState.ts`, as Compare's), the total first when picked (`opts.total`), on one grid with the chart module's `alignSeries()`: with `style: bars` by `barUnit()` (Charts' bars), with areas the shortest of 1 min … a week giving at most 150 steps (never under 6 h on a 6-hourly span). Drawn from the first row with a value (a sliver before a log's first point drew as zero). Areas go through each row's middle, the ends at the span's edges. The total, where picked, is a dashed line over the stack.
- Units: the first readable layer's set them; W, kW and MW (and Wh, kWh, MWh) are scaled onto them (`UNIT_SCALE`), a 0–1 `%` by the kit's `scaled()`; a layer in other units is left out, its chip muted with `Other units`. The pickers list power first (`listSlots(query, 'power')`).
- Settings (21, from `scripts/chart-settings.json`): `total` ("Total", optional, its hint), `slot1`…`slot6` (Compare's "Value N") each with its `colorN` (the kit's `COLORS` in order by default, as Timeline's), the time span block (default 24 hours), `style` ("Style": `area` "Areas", `bars`), `legend` (Charts'), `title` (a chip first in the legend), `showHeader`, `colorStyle` (Vivid: areas at 60 % against 30 %, bars at 90 % against 70 %; the lines keep their colours).
- The legend: a chip per layer (Other first), in the page's flow and wrapping (`flex-wrap`), above the plot or with `legend: below` under its labels, so the plot always starts `TOP_PAD` (10 px) into the svg (Charts' chips sit absolutely over a fixed 38 px, which a second line of chips would overlap).
- Header: the total now (the meter's value, or the layers' sum without one; `Total · Now`, or a finished span's last row's time) and the largest part's share of the span (`54 %`, `Largest · EV charger`, in its colour; the shares of the stack above zero, each row weighed by its length, Other included). A tap: the total at that row (`Total · 14:00`, a bar's `14:00–15:00`, a day's date, a weekday before it over several days) and the layer under the pointer (or the nearest), its value and its share of that moment (`Oven · 27 %`). A cursor line; the tapped time is kept, so it stays put as the window moves.
- Live: `values:state` onto the total and every layer with that value (not on 6-hourly spans); redrawn every minute; refetched every 5 min.
- `dev/stacked-preview.html` (`mock-stacked.js`: the showcase house's meter, heat pump, oven, EV charger and a solar inverter, on 5-minute marks), the preview frames `stk-dark`/`stk-light` (the prefix `sk` is the Smart Stack's), the README screenshot `stacked` (24 hours, Other, heat pump, oven, EV). `npm run test-dashboards -- stacked` (under Test charts). Rendered with Playwright's Chromium, as Timeline's. Not yet tried on the Homey or the phones.
