Widget Builder · mock-up 2

With your feedback: the parts library is now on the left (a search, groups, and a Layers tab for what's in the design); designs are picked from the title. Containers hold parts on a finer grid (12 columns, 8 px rows) or freely placed. Any part can be opened to Flows, one setting at a time, from the new Flow tab. The ⚡ badge marks parts a Flow can change. All data is made up (the house Solbakken).

1 · Builder: a container on the fine grid

The "Living room" container is 4×3 on the widget's grid; inside, its parts snap to 12 columns × 8 px. A part inside a container can drop its own tile (Look → Tile: off) and sit right on the background. Left: the parts library, grouped by Show, Control, Layout and Media, with a search, since there will be many.

2 · Builder: a free-form container and the Flow tab

"Entrance" is a camera picture as the background with parts placed freely on top: positions in % of the container, so it scales between 300 and 440 px. Pink lines snap a part to the edges and centres of the others, and the readout shows the position. On the right, the Flow tab of the "Message" text part: each setting a Flow may change is switched on one at a time and shows the card it adds.

3 · The Flow cards it adds

Only parts and settings opened to Flows appear in the cards' lists, named Design · Part. A value set by a Flow is kept by the app (as Flow Buttons' colours are) and reaches every copy of the design at once. A button or tile can also be a trigger instead of acting itself.
