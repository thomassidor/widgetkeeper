Widget Builder (mock-up)

Build a design from parts on a grid, save it, then place it on any web dashboard as the Widget Builder widget and choose the design in its settings. Changing a design updates every copy. Everything shown is made-up data from the house Solbakken.

1 · The builder page (#/builder/<design>)

Left: your saved designs. Middle: the design at its real size on a 4-column grid; drag a part from the bar to place it, drag its corner to resize, and use the switch above to preview it at 300 or 440 px, in dark or light. Right: settings for the selected part, with its rules at the bottom. The preview shows live values.

3 · The designs on a dashboard

Placed with Add widget → Widget Builder, then Design in its settings. The parts are the native tiles' look and the widgets' own (Device Values' tile, Quick Actions' switch, Light Controls' bar, Sparklines' line). In Garage the door's rule turns it red while it's open, and the "Car charging" part has a rule that hides it while the charger is off.
