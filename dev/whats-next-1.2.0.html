New widget

Chart · Totals

How much a value went up in each hour, day or month, like the Homey app's Energy tab: a meter's kWh or m³, a rain gauge's millimetres, hours of TV.

  • Compare with the period before: last week's, month's or year's bars beside this one's, with both totals at the top.
  • Utility meters that start again each month or year are counted on across the reset.
New widget

Chart · Scatter (XY)

One value against another, a dot per hour: does the heat pump work harder when it's colder? The trend line's slope says how much.

  • The newest dots strongest, so you see where it's heading.
  • A dot per minute to a week, by the time span. Tap one for its values and time.
New widget

Chart · Daily Profile

Today's line over the usual day: each hour's average over the last 7 days, dashed, with its spread as a band. Is today unusual?

  • The usual day from the last 7 or 3 days, today left out.
  • Now and the usual for this hour at the top; tap for another hour.
New widget

Chart · Min/Max

Each day's lowest to highest as a bar, with its average as a tick: how cold the nights and how warm the afternoons were.

  • The last four weeks, or any time span: a bar an hour over a day, a month over a year.
  • The latest bar's high and low at the top; tap a bar for its own.
New widget

Chart · Ranking

One value from up to eight devices, side by side and sorted: each room's temperature now as a dot, its range today as a bar. Which room is warmest?

  • Highest first, lowest first, or in the order you picked them.
Chart · Basic

Web dashboards

A live preview

While you set up a widget on a web dashboard, its settings show the widget as it will look, beside them (above them on a narrow screen), and it follows each change you make.

  • Devices included: pick another value, and the preview draws it.
  • Cancel leaves the widget as it was.
  • Every Widgetkeeper widget, the new charts too.
