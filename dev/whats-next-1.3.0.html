New widget

Combined Value

One value of several devices as one: the average temperature of every room, the house's total power, the lowest battery. A chart shows each device against the others.

  • An OK range such as 18–24 or <1000 turns the tile orange or red outside it, or counts who's outside.
  • Tap for every device, sorted, those outside marked.
  • Average, median, sum, lowest or highest. Pick no devices to count every device with the value.
  • A device in other units is left out of a sum or average, and the tile says so.
New widget

Value Board

Every value of the devices you pick in one widget, grouped by room or device, so a house's readings don't take a dozen Device Values widgets.

Or compact
  • What needs a look stands out by itself: a low battery, an open contact, an alarm, high CO₂. Fine values can go green.

Web dashboards

A side pane with your dashboards

Go from any dashboard to any other, also in full screen. It floats over the widgets, or stays open beside them on a wide screen.

  • Customize this list: hide dashboards and sections, drag them into this browser's own order, and tint the pane like the dashboard if you like.
  • All dashboards and Settings are at its foot, which folds away. Settings opens over any view, in tabs.
Web dashboards

Your own name and icons

Your home's name and icon in the header and the browser's title, the same in every browser, and an icon for each dashboard if you like.

  • One of the 201 icons, or a picture of your own.
  • A dashboard's icon shows in the side pane, on the front page and by its title.
Web dashboards' editor

Any background, and widgets that blend in

A colour of your own beside the preset tints, and a Widget background slider, so a white chart doesn't stand out on a tinted backdrop.

  • Per dashboard, and nothing changes until you move it.
  • Turned low, a thin line keeps the tiles' edges.
Chart widgets

Chart height and legend

Small, normal or tall on seven chart widgets, for a chart two columns wide. And six more can put the legend above or below, or hide it.

Web dashboards' editor

Editing loses no work

  • A failed save stays up with Try again and Discard, and Done waits for the saves.
  • Undo after deleting a dashboard or removing a column with widgets in it.
  • Changed settings take a second tap to throw away, and a new widget that's cancelled is gone.
Device widgets

Devices that drop off

A device Homey calls unavailable no longer looks live: dimmed, with a small ! in the corner.

And also
  • Cameras, Always live: pick one to play by itself and fill the widget when the dashboard opens.
  • Cameras refresh up to every 12 hours, and opening a dashboard doesn't wake a battery camera. Or only by Flow, with the card Refresh a camera's picture.
  • Popups on web dashboards: a camera's live video, and a size that fills the page.
  • An unavailable device's old reading doesn't count in Combined Value, Room Health and Value Matrix. A deleted one keeps its name in Device Values and Sparklines.
  • Quicker to recover: widgets try again after 5, 15 and 30 seconds, and read again when you come back to a dashboard.
  • Web dashboards update themselves after an app update, unless someone is editing or a dialog is open.
  • Flow Buttons: activate with a double or long press, so a stray tap can't start a Flow.
  • Rename tiles in Activity Dots (each sensor) and Value Board (its rooms and devices).
  • Green when fine in Value Matrix, Combined Value and Value Board.
  • A vibration on a tap that changes something in Light Controls, Lights by Room, Curtains, Thermostat Presets, Variable Presets and Variables.
  • Settings in one order: rename first, then what to show, the layout and display options, colour, then each tile. Nothing you've set changes.
  • The same words for the same settings: Smooth chart lines, Tiles per row (from the fewest), Name, Colour, Layout.
  • Smooth chart lines in Chart · Stacked and Sparklines; the numbers stay exact.
  • A colour for Sparklines and the Energy Price Strip's bars; any colour for the Electricity Overview's usage history.
  • Smart Stack: Combined Value and Flow Tiles can be its pages.
  • Web dashboards: all three new widgets, in the editor too.
