New widget

Combined Value

One value of several devices as one: the average temperature of every room, the house's total power, the lowest battery. A chart shows each device against the others.

  • An OK range such as 18–24 or <1000 turns the tile orange or red outside it, or counts who's outside.
  • Tap for every device, sorted, those outside marked.
  • Average, median, sum, lowest or highest. Pick no devices to count every device with the value.
  • A device in other units is left out of a sum or average, and the tile says so.
New widget

Value Board

Every value of the devices you pick in one widget, grouped by room or device, so a house's readings don't take a dozen Device Values widgets.

Or compact
  • What needs a look stands out by itself: a low battery, an open contact, an alarm, high CO₂. Fine values can go green.

Widget settings

Better pickers

Every list of devices and values searches by words, in any order, and shows what you'd be picking.

temp kitchen
Air sensor · TemperatureKitchen · 22°C
Fridge · TemperatureKitchen · 4.1°C
  • Each value's reading and glyph in the list, so two sensors are told apart.
  • Word starts first, and Spot 2 before Spot 10.
  • Lists say why they're empty: Pick a device first, No speakers found.
  • Long lists stop at 100 rows, then ask you to keep typing.
  • On web dashboards the current choice is marked, and arrows, Enter and Escape work.
Charts

A chart in your words

Chart · Lines, Stacked, Timeline and Ranking take a name per value, in place of the device's.

  • Hide the legend in Chart · Basic, Lines and Stacked.
  • Smooth chart lines in Chart · Stacked and Sparklines; the numbers stay exact.
  • A colour for Sparklines and the Energy Price Strip's bars; any colour for the Electricity Overview's usage history.
Tiles

Widen the last tile

At 2 per row, the last of an odd number fills the row: Device Quick Actions, Device Values, Curtains and Sensor Alarms.

Settings made alike, and also
  • Flow Buttons: activate with a double or long press, so a stray tap can't start a Flow.
  • Rename tiles in Activity Dots (each sensor) and Value Board (its rooms and devices).
  • Green when fine in Value Matrix, Combined Value and Value Board.
  • A vibration on a tap that changes something in Light Controls, Lights by Room, Curtains, Thermostat Presets, Variable Presets and Variables.
  • Settings in one order: rename first, then what to show, the layout and display options, colour, then each tile. Nothing you've set changes.
  • The same words for the same settings: Smooth chart lines, Tiles per row (from the fewest), Name, Colour, Layout.
  • Smart Stack: Combined Value and Flow Tiles can be its pages.
  • Web dashboards: all three new widgets, in the editor too.
